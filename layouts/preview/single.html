Alcohol & Mitochondrial Architecture

How ethanol reshapes mitochondrial structure in hepatocytes and drives the formation of megamitochondria in steatotic liver disease.

Chronic alcohol consumption profoundly disrupts mitochondrial morphology in hepatocytes. Our research investigates the molecular mechanisms underlying these structural changes, with a particular focus on megamitochondria — enlarged, dysfunctional mitochondria that serve as both biomarkers and potential therapeutic targets.

Using precision-cut liver slices from patients with alcohol-related liver disease, we characterise the dynamics of mitochondrial fusion, fission, and biogenesis in a physiologically relevant human model.
